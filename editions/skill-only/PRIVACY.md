# Privacy

gemkeeper collects no data. It has no servers, no telemetry, no analytics and no network access of its own.

The skill asks Claude to write notes about your work to `.gemkeeper/ledger.md` inside your project. That
file stays on your machine (or in your repository, if you choose to commit it). It is read and written
only by your own Claude sessions, under the same terms as the rest of your conversation with Claude.

The skill tells Claude never to write secrets, tokens or credentials into the ledger.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/adamoelz/gemkeeper/issues).
